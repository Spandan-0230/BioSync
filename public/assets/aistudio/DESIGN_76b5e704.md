## Brand & Style
The system serves scientific operators, orbital habitat crew, and bio-telemetry chronometrists synchronizing human circadian rhythms against non-terrestrial relativistic frames. The visual language rejects atmospheric softness, diffusion, and decorative skeuomorphism. It operates on an absolute OLED canvas where pure functional clarity, mathematical precision, and instant signal recognition are imperative.

The design movement is **Technical Constructivist Minimal**:
- **Pure Vector Rigor:** Monolithic hairline separators, 90-degree corners, and explicit coordinate grids.
- **Relativistic Data Density:** Instrument clusters, orbital phase gauges, photoperiod spectrum timelines, and telemetry status arrays presented with high legibility.
- **Sterile Void Contrast:** Critical biometric signals emerge from an unlit vacuum (#000000), eliminating cognitive noise and optical fatigue under continuous mission monitoring.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly against true OLED black to simulate vacuum spatial operational hardware:

- **Canvas Background:** Pure Pitch Black (`#000000`). No ambient tinting, grey lift, or base canvas gradients.
- **Primary / Hyper-Crisp Cyan (`#00f5d4`):** Real-time synchronization states, zero-drift telemetry, circadian phase lock, and primary active inputs.
- **Secondary / Cobalt Electric Blue (`#00a6fb`):** Orbital telemetry tracks, apogee/perigee markers, and sub-second hardware clock registers.
- **Tertiary / Solar Amber (`#ffb703`):** Relativistic drift warnings, photoperiod dawn/dusk transitions, and metabolic cycle phase boundaries.
- **Quaternary / Ionized Violet (`#9d4edd`):** Deep sleep cycle depths, dark-phase circadian resets, and deep orbital occultation zones.
- **Stark White (`#ffffff`):** Critical vector coordinates, primary numeric readings, and hairline optical division rules.
- **Muted Structural Neutral (`#11161b` / `#1c242c`):** Structural grid coordinate framing and inactive instrumentation slots.

## Typography
Typographic composition demands absolute precision and zero optical distortion:
- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Engineered, mechanical geometry for mission titles, time-sector headings, and system-level declarations.
- **Body Content (Geist):** Purely objective, high legibility for biometric logs, operational instructions, and physiological feedback.
- **Labels & Instrumentation (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced numeric tables, telemetry ticks, orbital period parameters, UTC/Mission Elapsed Time, and relativistic variance indicators. All numerals must render with tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate spatial shifting during high-speed sensor streams.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a strict constructivist technical grid modeled after spacecraft console instrumentation and optical reticles.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column modular grid on desktop (1440px+), collapsing to 6-column on tablet (768px-1024px), and 2-column or single-column stacked telemetry viewports on mobile (&lt;768px).
- **Hairline Axis Alignment:** Grid modules align directly to structural axes defined by 1px solid hairline dividers. Negative space is measured and deliberate, avoiding floating asymmetric islands.
- **Telemetry Enclosures:** Data clusters align edge-to-edge with shared 1px boundary lines rather than separated cards, preserving high information density across multi-display console setups.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created without blur, ambient drop-shadows, or glassmorphic refraction. Visual hierarchy is achieved entirely through line weight, surface contrast, and monochromatic vector stratification:

- **Zero Shadows:** `box-shadow` values are strictly `none`. Ambient diffusion is prohibited.
- **Layer Stacking via Opacities & Tiers:**
  - Base layer: `#000000` (deep vacuum).
  - Telemetry container fill: Solid `#05080a` or `#090e13` (low-reflectance instrument housing).
  - Active overlays / Modal HUDs: `#000000` backed with an impenetrable 1px perimeter border in `#00f5d4` or `#ffffff`.
- **Perimeter Reticles & Hairline Framing:** Containers use 1px solid borders (`#1a232b` for resting state, `#ffffff` for focused telemetry, and `#00f5d4` for active capture).
- **Corner Marks & Calibration Ticks:** High-priority cards feature 4px corner tick overlays or technical crosshairs to mark spatial coordinate boundaries.

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly non-filleted and razor-sharp (`roundedness: 0`).
- Every button, input, module, card, and popover terminates in an uncompromising 90-degree right angle (0px border-radius).
- Diagonal 45-degree chamfers (cut-corners) may be applied sparingly via CSS `clip-path` (e.g., `polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 8px) 0, 100% 8px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) to delineate primary execution triggers or bio-hazard lockdown alerts.
- Circular geometries are reserved strictly for planetary orbital tracks, 24-hour circadian chronometer dials, and vector direction indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Telemetry Triggers
- **Primary (Action / Engage):** Pure `#00f5d4` background with solid `#000000` text, uppercase `label-md` in `JetBrains Mono`. Zero roundedness. Hover state inverts to `#ffffff` background with `#000000` text.
- **Secondary (Subsystem):** Solid `#000000` background, 1px solid `#00a6fb` border, `#00a6fb` text. Hover shifts background to `#00a6fb` with `#000000` text.
- **Critical / Relativistic Abort:** Solid `#000000` background, 1px solid `#ffb703`, text in `#ffb703`.

### Telemetry Cards & Module Enclosures
- Pitch-black background (`#000000`) or deep carbon fill (`#05080a`).
- 1px hairline border in `#1a232b`.
- Top-right coordinate label (e.g., `SEC_04 // ORB_SYNC`) rendered in `label-sm` muted text.
- Optional 45-degree cut-corner notch on top-right corner.

### Inputs & Vector Fields
- Solid `#000000` ground with 1px border in `#24303b`.
- Text renders in `JetBrains Mono` stark white (`#ffffff`).
- Focus state instantly snaps border to `#00f5d4` with zero ease/blur transition.
- Unit labels (e.g., `ms`, `μg/dL`, `km/s`) pinned to the right edge in `label-sm` `#00a6fb`.

### Toggles, Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 14x14px square, 1px solid `#24303b`, filling with solid `#00f5d4` with a black center vector dot when active.
- **Radios:** Diamond-shaped (45-degree rotated square), 1px solid `#24303b`, inner solid diamond core illuminated in `#00a6fb` upon selection.
- **Toggles:** Stepped two-position rectangular track (0px radius) with an instantaneous jumping binary block; no continuous slide or fluid spring animation.

### Telemetry Chips & Photoperiod Status Tags
- Monospaced badges enclosed in 1px borders.
- **Active Light Sync:** Border and text `#ffb703`, background transparent.
- **Delta-Phase Dark:** Border and text `#9d4edd`, background transparent.
- **Nominal Circadian Track:** Border and text `#00f5d4`, background transparent.

### Relativistic Dilation Indicators & Chronometer Readouts
- Dual-dial or dual-bar arrays showing Habitat Elapsed Time (HET) vs. Earth Standard Time (EST).
- Micro-tick linear progress scales using 1px vertical interval ticks spaced across horizontal axes.
- Differential drift counter permanently locked in `telemetry-readout` format with leading zeros.
